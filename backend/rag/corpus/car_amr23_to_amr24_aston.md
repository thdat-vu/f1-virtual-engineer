The AMR23 (2023) opened the season as the second-fastest car after Red Bull and put Alonso on six early podiums. Development stalled mid-season and the car slid back to the midfield. The AMR24 (2024) inherited the development path but never recovered the launch-spec form — race-trim tyre wear regressed and the team finished a clear fifth in the constructors. A 2023 vs 2024 cross-year compare flatters the AMR24 in qualifying single-lap and exposes it in race-trim long-stint pace.
