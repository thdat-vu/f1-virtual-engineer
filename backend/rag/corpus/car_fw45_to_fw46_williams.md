The FW45 (2023) was the lowest-drag car on the grid, which let Albon score points at Monza, Spa, and Vegas despite a downforce deficit at high-load circuits. The FW46 (2024) kept the low-drag philosophy and switched the front suspension to a pull-rod layout to clean up the front-floor airflow. Cross-year deltas at street circuits are small; at high-downforce circuits like Hungary or Singapore the FW46 finds time the FW45 couldn't access. Race-trim tyre wear remained a structural weakness in both seasons.
