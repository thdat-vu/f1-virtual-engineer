The MCL60 launched poorly — McLaren said publicly it had missed its development targets — and the team backloaded an aggressive upgrade package for Austria 2023 (new floor, sidepod inlet redesign, beam wing). That upgrade transformed the car from Q3 contender to occasional podium threat. The MCL38 (2024) carried the Austria philosophy across the whole car, with revised front suspension to widen the working window. Cross-year deltas at the same circuit are large in slow-corner mid-throttle pace and high-speed downforce — Norris's 2024 Hungary win was unimaginable in MCL60 trim.
