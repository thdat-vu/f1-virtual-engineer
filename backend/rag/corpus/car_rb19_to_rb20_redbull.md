The RB19 (2023) was the dominant chassis of the post-2022 ground-effect era — wide downwash sidepods, narrow engine cover, and class-leading tyre management. The RB20 (2024) kept the philosophy but reshaped the inlet (overbite-style upper edge, bathtub depression on the upper deck) to feed the floor more cleanly at high yaw, and trimmed the engine cover further. Front suspension stayed pull-rod. Real-world delta showed up most in qualifying single-lap pace at high-speed circuits (Suzuka Sector 1, Imola Acque Minerali) — race-trim tyre wear advantage narrowed as Mercedes and McLaren caught up mid-season.
