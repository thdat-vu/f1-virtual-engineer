The SF-23 (2023) had qualifying pace but burned its tyres faster than Red Bull and Mercedes in race trim, and the porpoising at high-speed circuits forced the team to run higher ride heights than the floor wanted. The SF-24 (2024) reshaped the inlet sidepod and undercut profile, and the front-suspension geometry was reworked to reduce ride-height sensitivity. Race-trim tyre wear narrowed dramatically — Sainz's Australia 2024 win was a tyre-management drive the SF-23 couldn't have produced. Single-lap qualifying delta is small; race-pace delta is large.
