title: Mercedes W14 to W15 — zero-pod abandoned, conventional sidepods restored
source: F1 technical primer

topics: [generation, mercedes, w14, w15, sidepod, ride-height, 2023, 2024]
---

The W14 (2023) kept Mercedes' zero-sidepod concept that started with the W13. It produced bouncing, ride-height sensitivity, and inconsistent rear-end behaviour through medium-speed corners. The W15 (2024) abandoned the concept: conventional inlet sidepods, a redesigned floor edge, and a new front suspension geometry to widen the working ride-height window. Single-lap pace recovered most through long, loaded corners (Silverstone Copse, Suzuka Esses, Barcelona T3) where W14 had to lift off-throttle to manage porpoising. Straight-line pace is broadly comparable.
