---
id: drs-activation
title: Drag Reduction System (DRS) activation rules
source: FIA Formula 1 Sporting Regulations 2024, Article 22.1
section: "22.1 — Drag Reduction System"
topics: [drs, overtaking]
---

DRS may only be activated by a driver when they are within one second of the car ahead at the designated detection point. It is available from the end of the second racing lap and is disabled when the Race Director deems conditions unsafe — for example during rain, after a yellow flag in the DRS zone, or when the safety car has been deployed. Unauthorised DRS activation is reported by timing transponders and investigated by the stewards.
