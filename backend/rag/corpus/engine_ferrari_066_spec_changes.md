The 066/10 (2023) qualifying mode produced peak power but ran into reliability margins late in the season — Sainz's Singapore PU change was the visible symptom. The 066/12 (2024) rebuilt the qualifying-mode envelope around a longer-life turbocharger and revised MGU-K cooling, trading a small peak-power drop for the ability to hold qualifying mode through Q3 instead of dialling down on the warm-up lap. Cross-year same-driver Q3 lap delta at long-straight circuits (Monza, Baku) reflects this PU envelope change as much as chassis development. Race-trim deployment unchanged between specs.
