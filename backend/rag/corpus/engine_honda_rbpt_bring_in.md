Honda formally exited at the end of 2021. Red Bull Powertrains (RBPT) operates the same physical PU under the 2022-2025 development freeze, with HRC providing trackside support. Reliability fixes are allowed; performance development is not. Cross-year same-driver compares at the same circuit on Red Bull cars from 2022-2025 should show negligible PU-driven straight-line speed delta — any large delta is chassis or DRS-zone, not engine. The freeze ends with the 2026 PU regs, at which point all bets are off (see [[regulation-2026-power-unit]]).
