---
id: pit-lane-speed
title: Pit lane speed limit
source: FIA Formula 1 Sporting Regulations 2024, Article 34.7
section: "34.7 — Pit lane speed"
topics: [pit-lane, speed-limit, penalty]
---

The pit lane speed limit during practice, qualifying, sprint shootout, sprint and race sessions is 80 km/h, unless the Race Director specifies otherwise for a particular circuit. Any driver who exceeds this limit receives a financial penalty per km/h of excess during practice, and a time penalty during the race. Speed is measured by the FIA's electronic transponder system across the full length of the pit lane.
