title: 2022 ground-effect floor — return of venturi tunnels and porpoising
source: FIA technical regulations primer

The 2022 regulations replaced flat-floor downforce with shaped venturi tunnels under the car, generating most downforce from the floor instead of the upper-body wings. Goal: reduce dirty-air sensitivity so cars could follow more closely. Side effect was porpoising — the floor stalls when the rear ride-height drops below a threshold, the car rises, downforce returns, and the cycle repeats. Comparing 2021 vs 2022 telemetry at the same circuit shows large mid-corner speed shifts and very different braking points; trackside-edited kerbs (Australia, Imola) compound the layout-vs-pace ambiguity in any cross-year delta.
