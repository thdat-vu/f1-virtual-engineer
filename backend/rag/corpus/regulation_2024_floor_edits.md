The 2024 technical regs tightened the front-floor geometry and edge-wing height after teams had developed aggressive solutions through 2023 that the FIA judged outside the original ground-effect intent. Floor edge inboard rake was constrained, and plank stiffness checks were tightened. Effect was uneven across the grid: cars whose pace came from front-floor sealing (RB19) lost more than cars relying on rear-floor diffuser work. Cross-year deltas across the 2023→2024 boundary at high-load corners (Silverstone Maggotts, Suzuka 130R) reflect this regulation step, not pure car development.
