Mid-2025 the FIA halved the maximum allowed front-wing tip deflection under load and added a new asymmetric loading test. Teams had been running aero-elastic front wings that flexed at speed to reduce drag on straights and recovered downforce at slower corner speeds — McLaren and Mercedes were the clearest beneficiaries through the 2024 season and early 2025. Cross-year deltas across the test introduction (Spanish GP 2025) at high-speed circuits show the affected cars losing time on straights and gaining only partly back through corners. Use this when interpreting same-driver mid-2025 vs early-2025 deltas.
