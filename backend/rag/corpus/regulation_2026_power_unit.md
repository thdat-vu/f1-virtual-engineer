The 2026 rules split deployment roughly 50/50 between internal combustion and the MGU-K (up from ~20% electric), drop the MGU-H, and mandate 100% sustainable fuel. Chassis becomes shorter and narrower. Active aerodynamics replace DRS — driver-controlled flap on front and rear wings switches between high-downforce ("Z-mode") and low-drag ("X-mode") configurations on straights. Cross-year deltas at the same circuit between a 2025 and 2026 lap mix four effects: power-unit deployment shape, drag at top-speed, corner-entry downforce, and tyre construction changes. Pace deltas are not pure-chassis reads — surface that to viewers when comparing across the regulation boundary.
