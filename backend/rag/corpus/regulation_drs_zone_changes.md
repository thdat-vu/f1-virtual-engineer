DRS zone count and length is set per-circuit each season and is occasionally edited mid-event. Australia 2022 ran four DRS zones, the most ever; the FIA cut zone 4 (between turns 8-9) for safety after first practice and stayed at three zones in 2023-2025. Saudi Arabia ran three zones from 2021-2023 then expanded to four in 2024. Cross-year same-driver compares at these circuits show large straight-line speed deltas that are zone-count effects, not car-spec deltas. Surface this in the UI when comparing across a known zone change.
