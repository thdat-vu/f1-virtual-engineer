---
id: safety-car
title: Safety Car deployment and restart procedure
source: FIA Formula 1 Sporting Regulations 2024, Article 55
section: "55 — Safety Car"
topics: [safety-car, restart, procedure]
---

When the Safety Car is deployed, all competitors must reduce speed, form up behind the lead car, and may not overtake. Lapped cars may be permitted to un-lap themselves at the Race Director's discretion before the restart. The Safety Car returns to the pits at the end of the lap once the track is clear; overtaking is only permitted once the lead car crosses the Line and re-takes the lead after the SC1 board is withdrawn.
