title: Double-stack — pitting both team cars on the same lap
source: F1 strategy primer

A double-stack pits both cars from the same team on the same lap. The lead car pits first; the second car holds in the pit lane until the front mechanics are clear, then takes the same box. Cost: the second car loses 2-4 seconds versus a clean stop. Used when both drivers need the same Safety Car pit window or matching tyre offset. Risks: a slow front stop cascades onto the second car, and any unsafe-release or clash penalty hits the second driver hardest. Avoid if either car is in a fight with a rival on a different strategy.
