title: DRS train — when activation cancels itself in a queue
source: F1 strategy primer
section: Race-craft tactics
topics: [strategy, drs, traffic, overtaking]
---

A DRS train forms when three or more cars run within one second on a circuit with a long DRS zone: every car behind the leader gets the activation, so relative pace equalises and overtakes stall. The cure is creating a gap — either let the train drift to a 1.5-second buffer ahead of the next car so DRS only helps you, or pit early to drop into clean air and undercut the train back. Staying in the train past a single lap usually means giving up the position; track-position-only circuits like Monaco and Hungary make this strategy-defining.
