A free pit stop is any service that costs less than the gap to the car behind, so track position is preserved. The classic case is a Virtual Safety Car or full Safety Car — pit-loss collapses to 8-15 seconds versus a green-flag 20-25 seconds. Other free-stop windows: a comfortable 25-second gap to the car behind on a 22-second pit-loss circuit, or pitting on a lap when the chaser is also queuing behind a backmarker. Decision rule: take any free stop that mandatory-compound rules will eventually force you to take anyway.
