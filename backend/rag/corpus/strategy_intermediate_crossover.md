title: Intermediate crossover — the lap when slicks beat inters (and vice versa)

The crossover lap is the moment a drying track tips from intermediate-favoured to slick-favoured (or the reverse on a freshly wet track). Visual cues: a dry line forming through the racing groove, intermediate wear pattern showing graining, and the leading driver setting purple sectors despite drying conditions. Pit one lap before the consensus crossover to bank the out-lap on emerging grip. Pit one lap late and you bleed 2-4 seconds per lap on the wrong tyre. Wet-to-inter is harder — full wets only beat inters above ~3mm of standing water.
