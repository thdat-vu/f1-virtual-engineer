title: Overcut — stay out longer to leverage tyre life and clean air
source: F1 strategy primer
section: Tyre offset tactics
topics: [strategy, overcut, pit-window, tyre-offset, clean-air]
---

The overcut is the inverse of the undercut: stay out while the rival pits, run several laps in clean air on a worn but still-functional tyre, then pit later and rejoin ahead because your fresh-tyre out-lap is set on a clearer track than the rival's was. Effective when track evolution is high, pit-loss is large relative to fresh-tyre delta, or the rival's out-lap will be compromised by traffic. Weakness: requires the original tyre to retain enough pace — once the cliff is reached the strategy collapses.
