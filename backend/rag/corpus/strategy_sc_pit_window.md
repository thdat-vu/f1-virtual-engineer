title: Safety Car pit window — cheap stop while the field is neutralised
source: F1 strategy primer

A Safety Car neutralises the field at reduced pace, which compresses the time loss of a pit stop because the cars on track are also moving slowly. Pitting under SC typically costs 8-12 seconds versus a green-flag stop of 20-25 seconds. The trade-off: the pit lane queues — if more than two cars enter the same lap a stack-up costs the entire saving. Decision rule: pit immediately if you are in a one-stop window already, hold if your tyre offset would shrink on the restart, and never pit on the lap the SC peels in.
