title: Tyre cliff — non-linear pace drop on worn compounds

The cliff is the point on a worn tyre where lap-time degradation transitions from gradual (0.05-0.10 s/lap) to non-linear (0.5+ s/lap) within two or three laps. Triggered by thermal break-down, structural graining, or surface delamination depending on compound and circuit. Symptoms in telemetry: rear-tyre temperatures climbing, lock-ups under braking, and a sharp uptick in sector 3 time. Pit before the cliff or lose all pit-loss buffer in two laps. Soft compounds cliff the hardest; hards typically degrade gradually and rarely cliff.
