title: Undercut — pit early to gain track position on warm tyres
source: F1 strategy primer
section: Tyre offset tactics
topics: [strategy, undercut, pit-window, tyre-offset]
---

The undercut works when a chasing car pits one or two laps before the leader, fits a fresh compound, and uses the tyre-warmup window to set faster out-laps than the leader can match on worn rubber. The leader then loses time on out-lap traffic plus pit-loss and re-emerges behind. Effectiveness depends on tyre delta (a fresh medium typically opens 1.0-1.5 seconds per lap over a worn medium for the first two laps), pit-loss for the circuit, and whether the leader is in clean air or stuck behind backmarkers. Strongest on circuits with high tyre degradation and short pit-loss.
