---
id: tyre-compounds
title: Tyre compound allocation and mandatory use in a dry race
source: FIA Formula 1 Sporting Regulations 2024, Article 30
section: "30 — Tyres"
topics: [tyres, compound, strategy]
---

Pirelli allocates three dry-weather compounds per event: a Hard (C1-C3), Medium (C2-C4), and Soft (C3-C5), scaled to the expected circuit severity. During a dry race, each driver must use at least two different specifications of dry-weather tyres. If the race is declared wet and the driver has not completed the two-compound requirement, the rule is waived. Failure to comply without a waiver results in disqualification.
