Pirelli relabelled the compound range in 2023 (C1 became "harder than C1"), then rebuilt the construction in 2024 with stiffer sidewalls to address the 2023 graining issues and handle the increased downforce. 2025 added the C0 hardest compound for the most demanding circuits (Silverstone, Suzuka). Cross-year same-driver, same-event, same-tyre-name (e.g. "MEDIUM" 2023 vs 2024) is comparing two physically different tyres — not a like-for-like delta. Pace and degradation profiles shift accordingly. Surface this in the UI when the cross-year compare crosses a Pirelli construction year.
