---
id: yellow-flag
title: Yellow flag driver obligations
source: FIA International Sporting Code 2024, Appendix H Chapter 4
section: "Appendix H — 2.5.5.1.b Yellow flag"
topics: [flags, yellow-flag, safety]
---

A single yellow flag warns drivers of a hazard beside the track; double yellow flags signal a hazard on the track itself. Drivers must reduce speed, be prepared to change direction, and not overtake until they pass a green flag. Any driver who improves their lap time in a yellow-flag sector risks penalty — the stewards compare mini-sector times from the marshalling software to confirm compliance.
